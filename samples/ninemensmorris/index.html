Nine Men's Morris

Commentary

    White moves first (vs the computer you are White). Place your nine pieces, then slide them along the lines; with three left you may fly anywhere. Three in a row (a mill) removes an enemy piece. Reduce your opponent to two pieces or leave them without a move to win. 50 moves without a capture is a draw. A positive eval favours White.
