Chinese Checkers

Commentary

    Red moves first (vs the computer you are Red, starting at the bottom). Click a marble, then a highlighted hole. Step to a neighbouring hole, or jump over any single marble — chain as many jumps as you like. First to fill the opposite point of the star wins; a marble that has reached it may not leave. Holes are labelled by row a (bottom) to q (top) and column 1–25. A positive eval (in rows) favours Red.
